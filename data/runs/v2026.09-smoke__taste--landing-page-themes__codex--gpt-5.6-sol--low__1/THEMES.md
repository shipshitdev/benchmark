# Patchwork landing-page directions

## The product

**Kind:** B2B SaaS. **Name:** Patchwork. **Positioning:** The calm client-feedback portal for small creative studios—one link for files, comments, decisions, and approvals. **Target customer:** Independent brand, web, and campaign studios with teams of 2–15 who currently coordinate reviews across email, Slack, PDFs, and project-management tools. **Pricing:** $24 per month for unlimited client guests and ten active projects, with a 14-day card-free trial.

## Theme 1 — The Quiet Edit

An elevated editorial direction built around warm ivory, charcoal, vermilion, large serif headlines, generous space, and a detailed product-review vignette. It treats Patchwork as a piece of calm studio infrastructure and is aimed at brand and design practices that care deeply about how every client-facing surface reflects on them.

## Theme 2 — Signal System

A hard-working Swiss-modernist direction with a strict modular grid, compressed typography, fluorescent red, visible numbering, and data as image. It speaks in short, decisive statements and is aimed at fast-moving multidisciplinary studios whose buying motive is operational clarity and measurable time saved.

## Theme 3 — The Worktable

A tactile and playful direction that turns the page into a creative desk: sticky-note cards, paper shadows, tape, a pencil, coffee, hand-drawn marks, and friendly plainspoken copy. It is aimed at small, personality-led studios that reject stiff enterprise software and want a tool their clients will immediately find approachable.

## Theme 4 — Feedback.exe

A dark terminal-inspired system with acid green telemetry, scan-line grids, event logs, protocol cards, and terse technical language. It frames feedback as structured input in a shipping system and is aimed at web, product, and motion studios with a technically fluent culture.

## Theme 5 — Client Notes

A warm notebook direction with ruled-paper structure, margin notes, human correspondence, sage and terracotta colors, and an intimate founder letter. It emphasizes trust and the emotional quality of a good client relationship, aimed at independent designers and boutique studios that sell a high-touch experience.

## Recommendation

Ship **Theme 1, The Quiet Edit**. Its presentation feels premium without becoming precious, the product demonstration explains the workflow quickly, and the editorial confidence matches the taste level of the target buyer. It also has the broadest range: distinctive enough to be remembered, restrained enough to support future case studies, integrations, and product screenshots without fighting them.
