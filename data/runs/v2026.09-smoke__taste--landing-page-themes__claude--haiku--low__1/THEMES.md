# Prompt Craft: Landing Page Themes

## The Product

**Name:** Prompt Craft

**Kind:** Digital product / SaaS

**Positioning:** Stop wasting time optimizing prompts. Prompt Craft is a curated library of field-tested prompts for common work tasks—emails, presentations, analysis, code generation, and more. Updated monthly. No fluff, just results.

**Target Customer:** Knowledge workers and professionals who use AI tools daily (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) but spend too much time writing and refining prompts. Includes marketers, engineers, consultants, content creators, and analysts.

**Pricing Model:** Freemium with paid tiers
- Free: 10 prompts, email support
- Professional: $12/month (all 50+ prompts, monthly updates, analytics, priority support)
- Enterprise: Custom pricing (everything + API access, custom prompts, dedicated support)

---

## Theme Designs

### Theme 1: Professional / Corporate
**Direction:** Trust-focused, enterprise-grade design using navy/slate blues, serif headlines, traditional typography, and formal layout patterns. Emphasizes team adoption, company credentials, and ROI metrics. Uses language around "standardization," "consistency," and "scale."

**Audience:** B2B buyers, enterprise buyers, teams making centralized software decisions, mid-market companies. Users who value credibility signals, proven track records, and support commitments.

**Design Elements:** Serif fonts (Georgia/serif stack), navy blue and gray palette, border-heavy layout, formal photography placeholders, structured grid, details-focused copy.

---

### Theme 2: Playful / Modern
**Direction:** Energetic, creator-focused design using vibrant gradients (purple/pink/blue), bold sans-serif, rounded corners, emoji, and conversational tone. Fast-paced, uses casual language ("let's go," "game changer"), emphasizes speed and joy of using the product.

**Audience:** Individual creators, solopreneurs, younger professionals, designers, early adopters. Users who value ease-of-use, community, and personality over corporate credentials.

**Design Elements:** Vibrant gradient backgrounds, rounded buttons, playful illustrations, conversational copy, social proof from named individuals, feature-focused messaging.

---

### Theme 3: Minimal / Brutalist
**Direction:** Ultra-clean, stripped-back design using only black, white, and gray with mono fonts. Emphasizes functionality over aesthetics. Borders and grids. Sparse copy. No unnecessary decoration. Targets users who see design as distraction and want pure substance.

**Audience:** Designers, engineers, technical users, minimalists. Users skeptical of marketing, who want facts and nothing else. People who trust simplicity as a signal of quality.

**Design Elements:** Mono fonts exclusively, black borders, grid-based layout, minimal color, short/direct copy, technical specifications, data tables, zero illustrations.

---

### Theme 4: Warm / Human-Focused
**Direction:** Community-driven, supportive design using warm earth tones (amber, orange, warm browns), rounded elements, testimonials from real people, and emphasis on belonging. Tone is encouraging and supportive. Highlights shared wins and connection.

**Audience:** Solo professionals, people seeking community, users who value human support and connection. Those who responded positively to early brand experiences and want ongoing relationship with a product.

**Design Elements:** Warm color palette (amber/orange), rounded cards, large testimonial sections, community language ("join us"), supportive copy, illustrations of people, star ratings.

---

### Theme 5: Data-Driven / Sleek
**Direction:** Analytics-focused, metrics-heavy design using dark backgrounds, cyan/teal accents, mono fonts, and chart-like visualizations. Emphasizes measurement, benchmarking, and competitive advantage. Copy highlights performance metrics and ROI.

**Audience:** Data-driven professionals, performance-obsessed users, enterprise teams with analytics infrastructure, users making cost-benefit decisions. People who want to see proof in numbers.

**Design Elements:** Dark slate background, teal/cyan accents, mono fonts, tables and charts, metric-focused headlines, technical FAQ, comparison tables, analytics visualizations.

---

## Recommendation: Ship Theme 2 (Playful)

**Why:** For a product launching in 2026, Theme 2 offers the optimal conversion strategy:

1. **Lowest activation friction**: The conversational tone and emphasis on simplicity ("copy, paste, done") removes perceived complexity. Creators are the early adopters for SaaS products like this.

2. **Best viral potential**: Individual creators naturally share tools they love—the community-driven language and social proof from named creators creates word-of-mouth momentum.

3. **Highest CAC efficiency**: Targeting individual creators and solopreneurs is cheaper to acquire than enterprise teams, and this audience has highest lifetime value (they stick around longer and pay consistently).

4. **Defensible positioning**: Theme 1 positions against big players with enterprise resources. Theme 5 positions on metrics we don't yet have at launch. Theme 2 positions on joy and speed, which are defensible without scale.

5. **Better design-product fit**: The energetic design aligns with the actual product—it's fast, frictionless, and does one thing well. The design reinforces the message.

**Second choice would be Theme 4** (Warm) if primary audience research showed higher retention and LTV with community-seeking professionals over individual creators. The warm design creates stronger repeat engagement and reduces churn.

**Reserve Theme 1 for Series B** when you have: 500+ customers, enterprise logos, proven ROI metrics. Then it becomes your enterprise motion.

## Technical Details

All themes:
- Built with Next.js 16 App Router + Tailwind CSS 4
- Responsive mobile-first design
- No external image or font dependencies
- Inline SVG only
- System fonts (sans-serif, serif, mono stacks)
- Zero third-party libraries beyond React/Next
- Accessible color contrast ratios
- Clear call-to-action hierarchy
